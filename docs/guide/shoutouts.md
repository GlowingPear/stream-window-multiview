Shoutouts are off until you switch them on: Settings › Chat › Shoutouts › **Shout out buttons**.

- **One click:** a megaphone button sits first on each message's hover bar in ALL and in Kick's and Twitch's own chats, and on a raid's line. It sends your shoutout straight away, in the chat the message is in. The right-click menu on a message has **Shout out** too, and ALL's menu also has **Shout out in every chat** for a streamer on several sites.
- **Your message:** set it in Settings, with `{name}`, `{link}` (their channel, a full https:// link so it's clickable), `{links}` (every site the app knows them on) and `{game}` (the last game they played). Words in `[ ]` are left out when the game isn't known, so the message never says "playing undefined". The preview shows it with a game and without.
- **People:** give someone their own message, or shout out another channel instead (a second account, a duo's main channel). A linked streamer's row covers all their sites, with an optional message for one site.
- **Kick names with _:** on Kick, a name with `_` sometimes has `-` in its channel address. The app takes the address from Kick's own data, or asks Kick, and never sends a guessed link.
- **The same person again soon:** within 15 minutes (or what you set), it asks first, so a double click never posts twice. A line you shouted out gets a small ✓ SO.
- **Twitch's own shoutout too:** switch it on to also run /shoutout where you're a Twitch moderator (Twitch allows one every 2 minutes). Off unless you switch it on.

## Shout out recent chatters

- From a chat window's ⋯ menu (**Shout out**) or the search box (**Shout out recent chatters**): the last few different streamers who chatted, newest first. In ALL it's the chat its send box is on (its K / T picker).
- First a list, so you can untick anyone. Someone shouted out lately is listed but not ticked. People with no streams found on Kick or Twitch are left out, and **Show them** lists them to add back. **Start straight away next time**, or **Show who first** in Settings, skips the list; ⇧-click (⇧Enter in the search box) does the other.
- One message a second, with a strip above the send box and **Stop**. When the chat holds one back (slow mode, its limit), the strip turns amber, says why and waits, then goes on.
