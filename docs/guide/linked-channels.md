- Off until you turn it on: Settings › Linked channels › Link channels across sites. Off, every channel stands alone, as before. Title bars show each window's site icon either way.
- Link a streamer's channels with a suggestion (the same name on another site, or a link on their channel page), by dragging one row onto another in Channels, from a row's ▾ › Link another channel…, a window's ⋯ › Link channels…, their profile, or the search box (type "link"). Nothing is linked by itself.
- The Link channels card lists their channels (live or not), picks the main one (it comes first and gives the picture and name), adds a channel by site and name, @handle or link, and has this streamer's own choices: how their streams open, whether they open by themselves (together or separate) and the name shown.
- Channels shows a linked streamer once, with a small K / T / Y for each channel (dimmed when offline) and each live site's viewers. ▾ shows their channels, each with its own ⚡, bell and ✕. Counts are of streamers. The empty stage, the bell, Profiles and the search box show them once too.
- Their windows have a tab per site next to the name. Click one to watch that site; ⌥1–3 (Alt+1–3) or ⌥← / ⌥→ switch too, and ← / → once a tab has the keyboard. A dot means live, a white dot new messages, @ a mention and ! likely spam. The bar's buttons are those of the site you're watching (👍 only on YouTube).
- Tabs you're not watching keep playing, muted, and keep their automatic actions (YouTube's ad skip and auto-like, mentions, spam alerts, raids). On YouTube they play at the lowest quality until you watch them (Settings › Linked channels › Playing).
- Middle-click a tab, or right-click › Close this tab, to close just that one; + brings it back. The window's ✕ closes every tab. Drag a tab out (or right-click › Open in its own window) for a window of its own; its Join button puts it back.
- Chats: the chat follows the stream's tab, Tab in an empty chat box switches site (⇧Tab back; while you type, Tab completes names as the site does), and Side by side shows two or three chats in one window, a column each.
- ALL (every site's chat as one list): the bar's buttons that act on one site (the channel page and chat pop-ups, mod view, save, extensions, Inspect) ask which site, and save has Save all. In its ⋯ menu each site has a line of its own with those buttons; YouTube's has 👍 where the others have the mod view.
- When another of their channels goes live, its tab appears and glows. When the one you're watching ends and another is live, the window switches after 60 seconds (Stay on … switches back). It never switches back by itself.
- Raids: a raid from a tab you're not watching, or while another of their sites is still live, is a note under the bell with Open. The raid from the tab you were watching is followed once none of their sites is live; Back to … rebuilds their window.
- Turning it off keeps your linked streamers, your answers and the options, and splits their windows back into one per site.
