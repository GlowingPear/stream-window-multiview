- Off at first: switch it on in Settings > Support. When someone sends KICKs, bits or a Super Chat, gifts subs or memberships, subscribes, or raids in with their viewers (Kick and Twitch) in any open chat, that chat window glows, a pop-up says who and what, and it's listed under the bell (Mentions > Support).
- What alerts (Settings > Support): the smallest that alerts for each kind (e.g. 100 KICKs, 5 gifted subs or a raid of 50 viewers), and whether new subs and members count.
- How it alerts (Settings > Support): it also shows along the bottom of that channel's stream picture for about 10 seconds, in any layout (switch that off with Show it on the stream's picture). Pick the window's highlight and its colour, a sound, whether a pop-up shows in the app, and when to show a desktop notification. The pop-up, sound and desktop notification are the same settings as the Support row in Settings > Notifications.
- A community gift alerts once, not once for each sub it gave, and your own never alert you. It reads the chat's own notices, so it works without logging in.
