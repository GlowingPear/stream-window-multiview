- Auto-pause hidden streams (Settings > General): streams you can't see pause after 2 seconds and jump back to live when you can.
- Pop out streams: move every stream into a separate window (it opens on your second monitor if you have one). Chats stay here, and new channels go to the right window by themselves. In the streams window, **Tile** gives every stream equal space and **Spotlight** makes the stream you last clicked big; drag one stream onto another to swap them. "Bring streams back" or closing that window returns them.
- Pop out hidden (switch control): send only the streams you can't see to that window.
- **Several monitors** (Settings › Layouts › Monitors): for each layout, pick the monitor for its streams, its chats and the switch control. The chats stay in the main window, which moves to their monitor; streams on another monitor go in the Streams window there, and the switch control in its own window. Switching layout puts each part where that layout wants it, and a layout with nothing set leaves everything as you have it.
  - The picture shows your monitors as the system arranges them; **Identify monitors** shows each one's number on it for a moment.
  - "Put everything back on one monitor when one is unplugged" (on at first): when it's plugged in again, the windows go back to their monitors.
