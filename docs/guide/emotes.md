- Right-click an emote in any chat: the menu shows it bigger, with its name and where it's from (Kick, Twitch, YouTube, or an add-on like 7TV, BetterTTV or FrankerFaceZ).
- **Copy emote text** copies what to type in that chat to get the emote (its code, e.g. `KEKW`; on YouTube the emoji or its `:shortcode:`), ready to paste into the chat box. **Save image as…** saves the picture, named after the emote.
- A card when you point at an emote is off at first: switch it on in Settings > Chat > **Emote preview in chats** (point at the example there to see it). Move onto the card to use **Copy name**, **Copy image** (a still picture) or **Download**.
- **New emotes** (off at first: Settings > Chat > **Highlight new emotes**): emotes a channel added since you last opened its chat glow in that chat until you point at one, and a message names them when the chat opens. Works for Kick and Twitch, with 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ. The first time you open a channel there's nothing to compare with yet.
- **Chat over streams** (off at first: the chat-bubble button in the toolbar, or Settings > Chat > Show new chat messages over the streams): each stream's new chat messages show over it, at the bottom left, with their emotes: each for 6 seconds and at most 5 at once at first. Under the switch in Settings > Chat, set how long each stays (4 to 30 seconds), how many show at once (3 to 15), or keep them on screen until newer ones push them out. It works in every layout, also over streams popped out into their own window, so you don't miss anything with chats hidden or small. The chat button on a stream's bar turns it off for that stream only (remembered). Desktop app only.
- **Count new messages in chats you can't see** (off at first: Settings > Chat): a chat that's out of sight in any layout (hidden by Chat Stage or Chat Rail, scrolled out of the window, or covered by another window or a dialog) counts the messages others write until you can see it again. The number shows on its stream's picture in Chat Stage, its tab in Chat Rail, its row in the switch control and its stream's title bar. Desktop app only.
- **Channel name in chat boxes** (off at first: Settings > Chat): each chat box says which channel you're typing in instead of the site's "Send a message", so you don't post in the wrong chat. Under the switch, **Text in the box** sets the words around the name ({channel} is the name; empty is just the name), with suggestions and a sample. A box that says why you can't chat (followers-only, slow mode, sign in) keeps the site's words. Kick, Twitch and YouTube.
