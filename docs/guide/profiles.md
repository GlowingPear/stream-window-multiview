- Profiles lists your saved streamers. Search by name, nickname or tag, filter (Kick / Twitch / YouTube, Live now, Favourites, Has notes, Tagged) and sort.
- Type a name that isn't saved and click Look it up to find that name on Kick, Twitch and YouTube, then view or save it.
- Click a streamer for their profile. It also opens from the person button on a saved channel or on a stream's window.
- What the site says: picture, banner, about, followers, links, the live stream (title, game, viewers, when it started, chat rules) and subscriber badges.
- What the app records: each stream it sees while it's open (start, end, title, game, viewer counts once a minute) and a follower count each day. From that: live history, the "when they usually stream" chart, usual start time, average length, streak, average and peak viewers, top games and a viewer graph of the current or last stream.
- Kick and Twitch past broadcasts fill in streams the app didn't see (marked VOD), when the streamer keeps them. Each number says where it comes from, and the line under the numbers says since when the app has been tracking and how many streams it saw itself.
- Your notes, tags, a nickname and a favourite star are kept on this computer.
- Unfollow on Kick unfollows on kick.com itself (it asks first, and can also remove them from Saved). You need to be logged in to Kick in the app.
- Pictures, names and follower counts load by themselves, one channel at a time: a few seconds after you save or import a channel, when a saved channel goes live (at most once a day each), and slowly (one every 30 seconds) for saved channels the app hasn't looked at yet or for a week. Opening a profile or looking a name up asks the site right away. The history comes from the live checks the app already does.
