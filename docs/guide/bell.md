- The bell in the toolbar keeps everything the app tells you about: mentions, likely spam, saved channels going live, raids it followed, and the app's own messages ("Logged out of Twitch", "Saved layout", a saved emote, an update, a closed channel you can still undo).
- Its tabs: All, Mentions (@ Mentions, Replies, Words, and one chat), Spam (one chat; Show chat, Not spam, Add to bots, and Why? for the signs it found), Live (Went live, Raids, Opened by itself, and one site; Watch or Chat only, "Already open" or "Offline") and App (Accounts, Layouts, Windows, Downloads, Updates; Log in, Undo while it still works, Show in folder, What's new). Each tab shows its latest 30.
- The red number counts new mentions and spam (Settings > Notifications can make it everything but app messages, or nothing). What a tab shows counts as seen; new ones keep their mark until the next time.
- All notifications (at the bottom of the bell) opens every one kept (the last 500 of each kind): the same tabs, search by name, message or chat, Unread only, grouped by day. Click one to go to that chat; hover one to delete it. Mark all read, Clear all (in that tab). Closing it counts them all as seen.
- Do not disturb (Settings > Notifications, or the crossed-out bell at the top right of the bell's list and of All notifications): for a while or until you turn it off. Everything is still kept under the bell, with no pop-ups, sounds or desktop notifications.
