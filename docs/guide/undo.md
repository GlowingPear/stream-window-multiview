- The curved arrows in the toolbar undo and redo changes to your windows: opening, closing, moving, resizing, swapping and layouts.
- Keyboard: Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y to redo (on a Mac: ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z).
