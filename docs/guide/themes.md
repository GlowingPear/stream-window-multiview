- Open Settings > Themes.
- Pick a ready-made theme: Pear, Purple Night, Ocean, Sunset, Cherry, Neon Arcade, Retro Amber, Midnight Mono, Daylight (light), Paper (light), Hikari (blue and white) or Tron (princess) (pink).
- Change any colour with its colour box, or type a colour code like #3fb6ff. Changes show right away.
- **Window border** is the outline around the selected and big windows. It matches Accent until you change it, so you can tone it down without changing the buttons.
- If a colour you pick would make buttons hard to read, the app keeps their text readable and says why under the colours.
- Type a name and click **Save theme** to keep it under "Your themes". The × deletes one of yours.
- **Back to Pear** returns to the original colours.
