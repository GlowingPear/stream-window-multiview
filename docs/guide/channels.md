- Click ☆ on any window to save its channel, or use **☆ Open & save** in the Channels box. Click a saved channel to open it.
- Typing in the box also searches your saved channels.
- Suggestions from the channels you follow: while you type, **From your follows** lists up to 5 channels you follow on Kick, Twitch or YouTube that you haven't saved (live ones first, with a red dot and viewers). With the box empty, **Live now from your follows** shows who you follow that's live. Click one (or Enter on it) to open it, ☆ to save it.
  - They come from your follow lists: Kick's is read by itself while you're logged in to Kick; Twitch's and YouTube's from **Import follows**, then again at most once a day while you're logged in. Only names are kept, on this computer. A site you're logged out of shows none.
  - ⋯ > **Suggest channels you follow** turns them off and forgets the lists.
- Tabs: All, Kick, Twitch, YouTube. K / T / Y badges show each channel's site.
- Live channels are listed first, with viewers and title. The Channels button shows how many are live.
- Bell: desktop notification when that channel goes live.
- Lightning: open that channel automatically when it goes live, once per broadcast (if you close it, it won't reopen until they next go live).
- Import: add the channels you follow on Kick, Twitch or YouTube (you need to be logged in to that site in the app). For Kick, each channel in the import list also has Unfollow.
- Open live, Open all and Save all open are at the bottom.
- Person button on a row: that streamer's profile (see below).
