- Click the gear in the toolbar, or press Ctrl+, (Mac: ⌘,). It's also in the menu bar: on a Mac in the Stream Multiview menu, on Windows and Linux in File.
- **General**: start new streams muted, snap windows while you drag, auto-pause hidden streams, and keyboard shortcuts on or off.
- **Windows**: stream and chat windows' title bars: the channel's name on or off, and each button's place and whether it's always in the ⋯ menu, only when out of room, or never (one button, always next to ⋯). Drag the rows to order the buttons.
- **Accounts**: logins, Import follows, "Forget my login" and "Watch signed in".
- **Linked channels**: a streamer on several sites as one ([Linked channels](linked-channels.md)): the switch (off until you turn it on), then Tabs, Playing, Live & raids, Suggestions and Linked streamers.
- **Connected apps**: link with Pear Drops (see below).
- **Notifications**: Do not disturb at the top, then three tabs. **Alerts**, for each kind (mentions, likely spam, a saved channel going live, raids, the app's own messages): a pop-up in the app, a desktop notification (never, when the app isn't in front, or always) and a sound. **Bell & pop-ups**: what the red number on the bell counts and where pop-ups show. **Desktop**: desktop notifications on or off for everything, and a test to check your computer lets them through.
- **YouTube**: your YouTube login, skipping ads, YouTube's cookie question, liking streams automatically and the YouTube API key (see below).
- **Mentions**: your Kick and Twitch usernames, other words that mean you (nicknames), and how a chat window gets your attention when someone mentions you (see Alerts below).
- **Spam alerts**: for moderators (see below).
- **Themes**: the app's colours.
- **Search box**: the [search box](search.md) on or off, Recent on or off, and everything it remembers you ran, each with its × and Clear all.
- **Updates**: your version, checking for a new one, and what's new in each version.
- **Guide** and **Keyboard shortcuts**: this help.
- **Developer**: [Developer tools](developer-tools.md) and Copy diagnostics. The Help menu in the menu bar opens them too.
