- **Selected text:** Copy, **Translate to <your language>** (opens Google Translate in your browser with the text) and Search Google.
- **A link:** open it in your browser, or copy its address.
- **An image or emote:** open it in a new window, **Save image as…** (named after the emote), copy it, or copy its address.
- **A chat:** **Translate this chat to <your language>** translates the messages shown, each with a **TRANSLATED · <language>** badge (click it for the original). **Translate new messages too** keeps translating as messages come (remembered for that chat), and **Stop translating** puts them all back. To have every chat do this, switch on Settings > Chat > Translation > **Translate new messages in every chat** (off at first); Stop translating then turns it off in that chat only. Emotes, @names and links stay as they are. The messages are sent to Google Translate, and nothing is sent until you choose it.
- **The chat box:** Undo, Redo, Cut, Copy, Paste, Select all, and spelling suggestions for a misspelled word.
- **A message:** **Copy message** copies its text without who sent it, leaving emotes out; **Copy message with emotes** writes each emote as you'd type it.
- **Anywhere else:** Reload.
- With [Developer tools](developer-tools.md) on, a **Developer** group adds Copy message HTML, Copy chat HTML, Why didn't this alert? and Inspect.
