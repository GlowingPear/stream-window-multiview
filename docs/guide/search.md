Press **Ctrl+K** (on a Mac: **⌘K**) anywhere, even inside a chat or stream, or click the magnifier in the toolbar. Type a few letters and press **Enter**. Ctrl+K again, or **Esc**, closes it.

It finds:

- **Channels**: your saved channels (live ones first), what's open, the channels you follow, and any name or link you type or paste. A name nobody saved is **Open “xyz” as a channel**, with **K**, **T** and **Y** to pick the site (Tab for the next one, ⇧Tab the one before). Once a site says it exists, it's drawn big with its own buttons, unless a channel you know has a name like it: then that one stays big and the one found is a row under it.
- **Windows**: each open stream, chat and app window. Enter goes to it.
- **Layouts**: the built-in ones and your own, and Save layout as “…” with the words you typed.
- **Actions**: everything the toolbar, its menus and the keyboard shortcuts do, like Mute every stream, Lock layout, Pop out streams, Do not disturb, a theme, or logging in.
- **Settings**: every option. A switch flips right there; anything else opens its page with the option lit.
- **Help**: the guide's pages (with the words they say marked), and with **?** every keyboard shortcut.
- **Notifications**: recent mentions, spam alerts and channels that went live. Enter shows that chat.

**A channel's name**: type it, and that channel comes first, drawn bigger with what it's streaming and its own buttons (Stream + chat, Stream only, Profile, Save…, or Go to it, Make big, Mute… when it's open). **←** and **→** (or Tab and ⇧Tab) pick a button, Enter runs the one picked, and Ctrl+1–9 (⌘1–9) the others. **More…** lists everything for it.

After a layout from the box, the pop-up says so, with ⌘Z to undo it.

## Keys

- **↑ ↓** move (also Ctrl+N and Ctrl+P), **Enter** runs the row.
- **⇧Enter** the second choice: a setting's page, a stream made big, a channel's stream only.
- **Tab** and **⇧Tab** go forward and back: along a channel's buttons, a name's sites, the two columns of a row's list, or the rows. On a row with a list of its own (a window, a channel), Tab opens it: every action for it, in two columns.
- **Ctrl+1–9** (⌘1–9) runs a channel's button, or a row.
- **Esc** steps back: out of a row's list, then clears the words, then closes.
- **Del** (on a Mac also ⌘⌫) removes the Recent row you're on.

## Prefixes

Start with **>** for actions, **@** for channels and windows, **#** for Settings, or **?** for help (the guide, the keyboard shortcuts and Settings' options). On its own a prefix lists everything of that kind; Backspace in the empty box goes back to everything.

## Recent

With nothing typed, the box shows what you ran lately (**Recent**), the window you last clicked, and your saved channels that are live. What you use often comes higher.

- The row you're on has a **×** that removes it, and so does **Del** (⌘⌫ on a Mac). Plain Backspace never removes anything.
- **Clear all**, in Recent's heading, asks first, right there: Enter clears them, Esc keeps them. Typing *clear recent* does the same.
- **Manage** opens **Settings › Search box**, which lists everything remembered, each with its ×, and Clear all. There you can also switch **Recent** off (nothing new is remembered, and what's there is kept for when it's back on), or switch the **search box** off: the Search button goes from the toolbar, and Ctrl+K only says where to turn it back on.

Ctrl+K works even with the keyboard shortcuts switched off (Settings › General), so you can always find the switch to turn them back on.
