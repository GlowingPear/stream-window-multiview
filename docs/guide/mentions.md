- Mentions: when someone writes @you or replies to you in a Kick or Twitch chat, that chat window lights up and it's listed under the bell. Set your usernames in Settings > Mentions, or click Detect.
- Also alert me for (Settings > Mentions): add nicknames or other words that mean you. A message with one counts as a mention, with or without an @. Whole words only, any case: "pear" finds "hi Pear!" but not "pears". Your own messages never count. They're listed as "Word".
- Support (off at first: Settings > Mentions > Support): when someone sends KICKs, bits or a Super Chat, gifts subs or memberships, subscribes, or raids in with their viewers (Kick and Twitch) in any open chat, that chat window glows, a pop-up says who and what, and it's listed under the bell (Mentions > Support). Set the smallest that alerts for each kind (e.g. 100 KICKs, 5 gifted subs or a raid of 50 viewers), and whether new subs and members count. It also shows along the bottom of that channel's stream picture for about 10 seconds, in any layout (switch that off with Show it on the stream's picture). A community gift alerts once, not once for each sub it gave, and your own never alert you. It reads the chat's own notices, so it works without logging in.
- Clicking into a chat counts its mentions and spam as seen.
- To stop mentions from one chat, click the @ button on that chat window's bar (click it again to turn them back on).
- Settings > Mentions sets how: the window's border glows briefly or stays lit until you click that chat, and can get stronger while you haven't seen it (pulse, then flash). Pick its colour, a sound (or your own sound file), whether the sound repeats, whether a pop-up shows in the app, and when to show a desktop notification. The pop-up, sound and desktop notification are the same settings as the Mentions row in Settings > Notifications.
- Live alerts come from your saved channels while the app is running.

> **Tip:** An alert didn't come? With [Developer tools](developer-tools.md) on, right-click the message and choose **Why didn't this alert?**
