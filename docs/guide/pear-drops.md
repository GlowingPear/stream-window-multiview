- Pear Drops is a separate free app that collects your Kick emote and badge drops by watching with Kick's own player. Stream Multiview can link with it, on this computer only: nothing goes online.
- Settings > Connected apps: switch on **Connect with Pear Drops**, and the same switch in Pear Drops. The first time, the two apps pair; after that they find each other whenever both are open. The card says how the link is: Connected, Waiting (Pear Drops isn't open, or its switch is off), Pair again, Can't connect (one of them needs updating) or Not installed (with a Download button).
- Why: Kick credits drop watch time to one stream per account. A Kick stream here that plays signed in to the same account takes the credit away from Pear Drops.
- **Sign Kick players out while Pear Drops is farming** (on by default): while Pear Drops farms, every Kick stream here plays signed out, and comes back signed in 5 minutes after it stops (it often waits a moment between streams, and every change restarts the players). Chats always stay signed in. If Pear Drops farms with a different Kick account than yours, nothing changes.
- While this is on, Settings > Accounts shows Kick's "Watch signed in" as a locked **Kick · Auto** chip: Pear Drops sets it. Switching it off (or Connect, or Disconnect) gives back your own Kick choice.
- **Show what Pear Drops is farming in the toolbar**: a chip next to Profiles with the channel and the drop's progress. Point at it for the next reward, every reward and when they unlock; click it to bring Pear Drops to the front.
- **Test** checks the link right now and says how it went ("Pear Drops 0.1.6 answered in 2 ms", or why not). **Disconnect** unpairs the two apps and switches the link off.
- Pear Drops is told whether the Kick players here are signed in, your Kick username and which Kick streams are open, so it can warn when they would take its credit. Never your login.
