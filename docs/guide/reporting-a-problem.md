1. Open Settings › Developer and press **Copy diagnostics**.
2. Make it happen again if you can, then copy them again: the newest errors are at the top.
3. Open an issue on the project's GitHub page (**Issues › New issue › Something isn't working**) and paste them in the **Diagnostics** box. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
4. **A chat looks wrong, or a message didn't alert?** Turn on **Developer tools** (Settings › Developer), right-click the message and choose **Copy message HTML**, **Copy chat HTML** or **Why didn't this alert?**, and paste that too.
5. **A stream is stuck, silent or says it's offline?** Choose **Copy window state** from its window's ⋯ menu, and paste that too.

- A screenshot or a short video helps.
- Nothing is sent by the app: you choose what to paste. Logins, cookies and keys are left out; channel and chat names can be in it, so look it over first.
