# Stream Multiview guide

How to use every part of the app. The same pages are in the app: Settings › Guide (F1 shows the keyboard shortcuts), where you can search them.

## Start
- [Getting started](getting-started.md): Install the app, open it the first time, and find your way around.
- [Search and actions](search.md): One box that finds and does anything in the app: press Ctrl+K anywhere and type a few letters.
- [Adding streams](adding-streams.md): Open a channel by name or link, from Kick, Twitch or YouTube.
- [Channels](channels.md): Save the channels you watch, see who's live, and get them opened or announced when they go live.
- [Streamer profiles](profiles.md): What each streamer's site says, when they usually stream, their past streams and your own notes.
- [Linked accounts](linked-accounts.md): One streamer on Kick, Twitch and YouTube as one: one row in Channels, one window for their streams and one for their chats, a tab per site.

## Watching
- [Moving windows](moving-windows.md): Drag, resize and swap windows, and what the buttons on a window's title bar do.
- [Layouts](layouts.md): Arrange every window at once: Focus view, Columns, Chat Stage, Chat Rail, Chat focus, Tile and your own.
- [The switch control](switch-control.md): One panel for every stream: pick the big one, mute, pause, preview and swap.
- [Playing a game](game-focus.md): Game focus: your game big, streams and chats beside it.
- [Another app's window](app-window.md): Show Discord or any other window right inside the multiview.
- [Saving bandwidth and monitors](saving-bandwidth.md): Pause what you can't see, pop streams out to another window, and put each part on its own monitor.
- [Undo and redo](undo.md): Undo and redo changes to your windows.
- [Other handy things](handy.md): Chat text size, where the toolbar sits, hiding it, full screen and more.

## Chat
- [Right-click in a chat or stream](right-click.md): Copy, translate, search, save emotes and copy a message from the right-click menu.
- [Emotes and chat extras](emotes.md): Emote preview, new emotes, chat over the streams, counting messages you can't see, and the channel's name in each chat box.
- [Kick's /clip, /poll and /prediction](kick-commands.md): Kick's own commands in the app's Kick chats.

## Alerts
- [Notifications (the bell)](bell.md): Everything the app tells you about, kept under the bell.
- [Mentions](mentions.md): Know when someone mentions you or replies to you in an open chat.
- [Support alerts](support-alerts.md): Know when someone sends KICKs, bits or a Super Chat, gifts subs, subscribes or raids in an open chat.
- [Spam alerts](spam-alerts.md): Spots promo-bot messages in chats you moderate, tags them and lights up the window. It never hides, deletes or reports anything.

- [Chrome extensions](extensions.md): Add Chrome extensions to chats and streams.
- [YouTube](youtube.md): Skipping ads, YouTube's cookie question, liking streams and the optional API key.
