      Spam alerts Beta

      A moderator tool: spots promo-bot messages and abuse in chats you moderate and points you to them, so you can deal with them. It never hides, deletes or reports anything.

      Also flag abuseInsults aimed at someone ("ur a …"), slurs and threats, and someone swearing over and over. Tagged "ABUSIVE".

      Tag the message in chatAdds "LIKELY SPAM" (or "ABUSIVE") after the message. Hover the tag to see why.

      A message is only flagged when several signs add up. One sign alone is never enough, so normal links and clip links aren't flagged, and a message needs a link, a name to add on another app (or to be posted in several chats) to count. The exceptions are a wall of the same word and a known bot line, which are flagged on their own.
      • Disguised link to a site not on the allowed list, written so the chat doesn't turn it into a link: site[.]com, site dot com, site . live, or site com with just a space (only for endings like .com, .net and .org that aren't everyday words, so "call me" or "going live" don't count) (3 points). A normal link to a site not on the list is 1 point.
      • Asks to be added off-site with a name, like "add me up on discord bram209" or "dm me on telegram: x_y", so the scam carries on where you can't see it (4 points: with a new chatter that's enough on Balanced)
      • Promo words like "upgrades", "viewers" or "zero cost" (1 point each, up to 2)
      • Emotes or junk breaking up the words (1 point)
      • The same message in another open chat in the last 5 minutes (3 points, the strongest sign)
      • A new chatter: started chatting in the last few minutes, often with friendly filler lines first like "Don't work too hard" (1 point)
      • The same word over and over, glued together like WordWordWordWord (10+ times) or spaced out (25+ times). Laughing and hype like hahaha or KEKW KEKW don't count (6 points, enough on its own)
      • Messages from accounts on the chat bot list in Lists (like BotRix or Nightbot) are never checked, and don't count as "the same message in another open chat" either.
      • A known bot line from the list in Lists, like "Hey stranger, still running the show?", the greeting some promo bots start with before posting their link, or "type !noads", the opt-out line under their ads (6 points, enough on its own)
      With Also flag abuse on (in Alerts), these are tagged "ABUSIVE". Words are found however they're written: stretched (whooore), with digits or symbols for letters (wh0re, b!tch, f*ck) or spelt out (c u n t).
      • An insult aimed at someone: "you", "ur a", "your a" or "you're a", then a word from Abuse words in Lists, like "ur a whore" or "you stupid little b!tch" (4 points: with a new chatter, or a second time, that's enough on Balanced). On its own a word like that is often banter, so it's not counted.
      • A slur or threat from Slurs and threats in Lists, anywhere in the message, like "kys" (5 points, enough on its own on Balanced)
      • Swearing over and over: the third message in two minutes from the same person that's mostly words from Swear words in Lists (5 points). One "holy shit" at a hype moment doesn't count.
      • Keeps at it: an insult or slur from someone who already sent one in the last 5 minutes (3 points)
      These are the built-in points: change them in Scoring.

      Sign in to YouTube

      YouTube sign-in currently only works through the Firefox browser. Google blocks signing in inside apps like this one, and Chrome and Edge lock their logins so they can't be copied.

      You don't need to sign in to watch YouTube streams. Signing in is only for typing in YouTube chat, importing the channels you follow, and "Watch signed in".

      1. Install Firefox from firefox.com if you don't have it.
      2. In Firefox, go to youtube.com and sign in.
      3. Click Copy my login from Firefox below. The app copies only your YouTube/Google login straight from Firefox and keeps it encrypted. No extension, no files.
      If you sign out of YouTube in Firefox later, the app is signed out too: sign in there again and click the button again. The same if YouTube asks Firefox to choose your account, or Import follows says the app is signed out: choose it in Firefox, then click the button again.
